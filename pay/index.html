How to enter shifts

One box per day. Type it however you say it.
  • 9-5 → 9:00pm → 5:00am (8h) — PM is assumed, and the end rolls into the next morning automatically.
  • 6pm-2am, 1800-0230, 9:30pm - 5:15 — all fine. Use -, to, or –.
  • 9am-5 → 9:00am → 5:00pm. Adding am flips it back to a day shift.
  • Two shifts in one day? Separate with a comma: 10am-2pm, 8pm-1am.
  • The shift is filed under the day it starts, even when it finishes at 5am the next morning.
  • brk is the unpaid break in minutes — it's taken off pro-rata across the shift.
  • PH marks that day as a public holiday.
How the pay is worked out
  • Every hour is paid at your base rate × the multiplier for that day: Mon–Fri 125%, Sat 150%, Sun 175%, public holiday 225%.
  • On top of that, weekday hours falling in the afternoon window (6pm–midnight) earn the afternoon penalty per hour, and hours in the evening/night window (midnight–6am) earn the evening penalty.
  • Weekend and public-holiday hours get the higher multiplier instead of the penalties (change this in ⚙ Rates).
  • Tax is an estimate of PAYG withholding at scale 2 (tax-free threshold claimed, no HELP debt). Super is a % of gross.
